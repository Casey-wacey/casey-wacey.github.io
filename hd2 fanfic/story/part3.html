Pt 3: Stare

Date: 22/08/2186
Location: Afoyay Bay, low orbit
Ship: SES Queen of Pride

I caught myself just staring at the DSS today,
don’t know for how long but long enough for the crew to notice me not moving.
The sounds almost seemed to stop when I was looking,
though I was extremely zoned out so they may have still been there,
I just didn’t notice.

The crew was celebrating the collapse of the void today.
Decided to join them, not like I had anything else to do.
It was fun. Don’t really talk to them a lot.

I wonder if any other Helldivers in orbit here are
experiencing the same thing as me?
