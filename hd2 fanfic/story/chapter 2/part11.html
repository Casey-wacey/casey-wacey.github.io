Part 11: Alive

Date: 24/08/2186
Location: Calypso(?), surface
Helldiver: John, [REDACTED] Regiment

I slowly open my eyes, I can feel a soft breeze on my cheeks. Is my helmet cracked?

Actually, I have a better question: how am I alive?

I roll onto my back, taking off my helmet — or at least what’s left of it.

I look to the side, at the horizon. The sun is setting, its light shining into my eyes.

Why am I alive?
