Part 13: Starry Night

Date: 24/08/2186
Location: Calypso(?), surface
Helldiver: John, [REDACTED] Regiment

Well I have. Especially now that there’s nothing else to do.

Anything with a screen got destroyed in the explosion.

My best guess is that my vital monitor got destroyed,
and thus they thought I was dead. Just a theory though.

The stars look magnificent, I can easily see the Milky Way from here.
All those stars, all the planets.

Humanity has finally reached the stars,
which we have gazed and wondered about for generations apon generations.

And what is our main use for these FTL drives you may ask?

War.

In fact, FTL drives are highly restricted outside of military use…

If only we could just explore the endless skies in peace. Those skies unknown.
