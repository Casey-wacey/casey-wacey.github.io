Part 1: The jump

Date: 20/08/2186
Location: Afoyay Bay, low orbit
Ship: SES Queen of Pride

Calypso is gone.

We had lost contact with the crew of the DSS, shortly after, it jumped to Calypso.
Apon high command gaining remote control of the station and warping it to Afoyay Bay for inspection,
the planet disappeared.

My ship was one of the few hundred tasked with orbiting near the DSS while the dissidents cleared it out
and logged everything.
It’s been a few days now, we successfully collapsed the void!
But I keep hearing this… knocking… its loudest on the observation deck. I keep telling myself it’s nothing, but apparently there were reports of knocking on the DSS before we lost contact.
So I’m starting to doubt myself.

Sigh

Just 3 more days until I’m shipped off to the automatons.
Can’t wait, love killing some bots
