Part 2: Denial

Date: 21/08/2186 Location: Afoyay Bay, low orbit Ship: SES Queen of Pride

Ok I can’t ignore it anymore.
I swear the knocking is getting so loud, I can hear it from anywhere.
I asked the crew and they said they don’t hear anything.
I swear I’m going insane.

It’s still the loudest near the observation deck.
Hell I’d say it’s unbearably loud there.

I tried to record the sound but nothing.
Maybe being on the ship for so long without combat is just what’s doing it.

2 more days man.
Then I’m out of this hellhole of a planet
